Aurora Commerce

A high-conversion storefront with a headless checkout and a design system built to scale from ten products to ten thousand.

Client: Aurora Retail Year: 2026 Scope: Design + Build Timeline: 6 weeks
Aurora Commerce

Overview

Aurora Retail had outgrown a template store that looked like every competitor and loaded slowly on mobile. They needed a storefront that felt premium, converted on the first visit and could grow with a rapidly expanding catalogue.

We rebuilt the experience from the ground up: a confident visual identity, a component-driven design system and a fast headless front end connected to a flexible commerce backend.

The challenge

Two thirds of Aurora's traffic came from mobile, where the old site took nearly six seconds to become interactive. Cart abandonment was high and product discovery was almost entirely search-dependent, with weak category storytelling.

The outcome

The new storefront launched with a three-step checkout, editorial category pages and a reusable merchandising system the Aurora team can update without developers. It is now the template for their regional rollouts.
