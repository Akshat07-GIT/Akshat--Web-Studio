Vertex AI Platform

An AI workspace that makes complex model workflows understandable — and approachable — for non-technical teams.

Client: Vertex Labs Year: 2026 Scope: Product Design + Build Timeline: 12 weeks
Vertex AI

Overview

Vertex Labs had built a capable AI engine but only engineers could use it. Their goal was a product that marketing, operations and research teams could run without writing code — while keeping power users happy.

We designed a visual workflow builder with plain-language steps, live previews and guardrails that prevent expensive mistakes, then built the front end and connected it to their APIs.

The challenge

AI tools often overwhelm with options. We had to expose real capability without a wall of settings, keep long-running jobs understandable and make results easy to verify and export. Performance with large datasets was critical.

The outcome

Vertex now ships a product non-technical teams adopt on day one, with a component library that lets the internal team add new workflow nodes quickly. Support requests dropped sharply after launch.
