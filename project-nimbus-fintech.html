Nimbus Finance

A calm, trustworthy banking dashboard that turns dense financial data into clear next steps for everyday users.

Client: Nimbus Bank Year: 2026 Scope: UX Research, UI Design Timeline: 8 weeks
Nimbus Finance

Overview

Nimbus Bank had powerful features buried under a dense interface built for analysts, not customers. Support tickets about "where do I find..." were surging and new users abandoned setup halfway through.

We ran research with existing customers, mapped the journeys that mattered most and redesigned the dashboard around three questions people actually ask: what is my money doing, what needs my attention and what should I do next.

The challenge

Financial data is naturally complex. The old interface showed everything at once with equal weight, so nothing stood out. Trust was also fragile: users needed reassurance about security and clarity about fees without wading through jargon.

+41%Feature adoption
-52%Support tickets
4.8/5Usability score

The outcome

The redesigned dashboard uses progressive disclosure, plain-language summaries and a consistent visual system. New users reach their first meaningful action in under two minutes, and the design language now guides every future Nimbus release.
