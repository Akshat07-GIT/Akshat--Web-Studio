How we work

Six phases. Zero guesswork.

Every project follows the same clear rhythm, so you always know what is happening, what is next and what we need from you.

01
Discovery

Goals, audience and constraints

We start with a focused conversation about what success looks like, who you are speaking to and what already exists. You receive a written summary so we are aligned from day one.

02
Strategy & scope

Define the smallest winning scope

We map pages, features and user flows, then agree on a prioritised scope with fixed pricing and a realistic timeline. No vague estimates, no scope creep.

03
Design

Wireframes to polished UI

Structure comes first, then visual direction. You review clickable prototypes and design previews, and we refine together until every screen feels right.

04
Build

Clean, tested, accessible code

We implement the approved design with performance, responsive behaviour and accessibility baked in. You get staging links to review progress as it happens.

05
Launch

Ship with confidence

We run final testing across devices and browsers, check every link, form and interaction, then deploy to your domain and monitor the launch.

06
Support

Handover and iteration

You receive documentation and a walkthrough. After launch we stay available for improvements, new pages and the next phase of growth.

What you get

Deliverables at every step.

Project brief

A written summary of goals, scope and success metrics agreed before work starts.

Design files

Responsive screens and a reusable component system you fully own.

Production code

Clean, documented source with no lock-in and straightforward handover.

Launch checklist

Cross-device QA, performance checks, SEO setup and analytics verification.

Handover guide

Post-launch support

An open line for fixes and improvements after your site or app goes live.
